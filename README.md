A single one-line cat joke with a setup and a turn that lands — no pun you've heard a hundred times, no explanation after the punchline.

## The joke

> I told my cat he's adopted; he blinked once, sat on my laptop, and changed my password.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the joke to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), guarded to the competition's 1–200 character gate and to a single line.
